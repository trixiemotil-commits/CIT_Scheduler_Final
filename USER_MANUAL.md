# CIT Scheduler User Manual

This manual explains the features available to students, teachers, and administrators in CIT Scheduler. Your account role determines which portal and actions are available. Some actions may also depend on your account permissions and on whether an administrator has set up and published the current academic term.

## 1. Getting started

### Sign in

1. Open CIT Scheduler and choose **Sign in**.
2. Enter your account email and password, then select **Login**.
3. Complete any verification shown on screen. Depending on the account and device, this may include a CAPTCHA, a mobile math check, or a six-digit email verification code.
4. If prompted for a code, check the email address linked to your account and enter the code before it expires.
5. Select **Remember me** only on a device you trust.

The system opens the portal for your active role. If your account has more than one role, use **Switch Role** in the portal navigation to change roles. The available roles depend on your account.

### Student registration

Public sign-up is for students. Select **Sign up**, enter your name, student ID, institutional email, and a password, then confirm the password and submit. The password must contain at least eight characters, including uppercase and lowercase letters, a number, and a special character. Complete any verification shown.

Teacher and administrator accounts are managed by administrators; they are not created through student sign-up.

### Navigation

- **Student portal:** use the bottom navigation for Home, Teachers, Consultations, Events, and Profile. Tapping **Consultations** opens a choice to view your sessions or book a consultation. Use your profile to reach account settings, FAQs, devices, and password controls.
- **Teacher portal:** use the sidebar for Dashboard, Schedule, Events, Consultation, and Settings. Your profile is accessible from your avatar.
- **Admin portal:** use the sidebar for Dashboard, View Schedules, Add Schedule, Academic Terms, Teachers, Events, Users, Activity Logs, and Settings. Some sidebar links may be collapsed behind the menu toggle.

Use **Logout** when you have finished, especially on a shared device. If a confirmation appears, confirm to end your session.

## 2. Students

### 2.1 Dashboard

The dashboard is your starting point. It summarizes teacher availability, recent consultation requests and their statuses, and recent events. Select a teacher-availability card or **Browse Teachers** to open the teacher directory. Select **My Sessions** or **View Consultation Session** to manage bookings. Select **View All Events** to browse announcements.

The notification icon opens your notifications. A badge indicates that unread items are available. On mobile, pull down on supported pages to refresh their displayed data.

### 2.2 Academic-term enrollment prompt

When an administrator publishes a new semester, the system may ask you to identify your enrollment for that term. Complete the prompt so your student schedules can be shown for the right group:

1. Choose **Regular** or **Irregular** enrollment.
2. For **Regular**, choose your year level and section.
3. For **Irregular**, select every year level in which you are enrolled, then select a section for each selected year.
4. Submit the form.

The offered years and sections come from the published term. If your correct option is missing, contact an administrator rather than choosing an unrelated section.

### 2.3 Find teachers and request a consultation

1. Open **Teachers** or choose **Book Consultation** from the Consultations menu.
2. Search by teacher name or subject, and use the available filters to narrow the list.
3. Select the teacher or open the booking form.
4. Choose a **Teacher Type**:
   - **Subject Teacher:** choose one of that teacher’s subjects and an assigned consultation time.
   - **Available Teacher:** choose an available teacher. Subject and assigned-time fields may not be shown for this type.
5. Choose a reason. Add a short description if useful. A description is required for **Other Academic Concern**.
6. Select **Send Request**. The form will not submit until required choices are complete and the selected teacher can accept the request.

Teacher cards may show their current status, subjects, and consultation availability. If no teachers are available for a type, try again later or contact the appropriate office.

### 2.4 Manage consultation sessions

Open **Consultations** and choose **View Consultation Session**. Use the status tabs to find the relevant request.

| Status | What it means | Available actions |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for the teacher to review the request | Edit the request or cancel it |
| Approved | The teacher approved the request | View session details and the queue ticket |
| Reschedule | A new time or follow-up action is needed | Accept a proposed time, choose another time where offered, or cancel |
| Completed | The session has been marked complete | View session notes |

For an approved session, **View Details** shows the confirmed session information and queue position. Use **Download Queue Details** in the details window to save the queue information.

If the teacher proposes a new time, select **Proceed with new time** to accept it or cancel the booking. If the request is in reschedule status without a pending proposal, choose **Reschedule** to update the request or cancel it. Review the details before confirming any changes.

### 2.5 Events

Open **Events** to view school announcements. Search by event text and switch between **Active** and **Ended**. Select an event card to view its details, including any published date, time, location, description, or cover image. Ended events remain available for reference.

### 2.6 Profile, notifications, and student help

- **Profile:** review your displayed account details and, where editable, update your profile information or picture. Use the profile menu for password, recent devices, FAQs, and logout.
- **Notifications:** open the bell from the dashboard to review account and consultation updates. Unread notifications are marked. Where shown, select a notification to open its related information.
- **FAQs:** open **FAQs** from your profile or settings for answers about account settings, email verification, and finding schedules.
- **Settings:** change your password or manage email verification. These options are described in [Account, security, and common help](#5-account-security-and-common-help).

## 3. Teachers

### 3.1 Dashboard and notifications

The dashboard provides a notification center for new activity, including consultation updates. Use the **All**, **Unread**, and **Read** filters to find items. Select a notification to open its related request or information. **Mark all read** clears the unread state; **Clear All** removes the listed notifications after confirmation.

The sidebar status indicator reflects your current availability/status. Use the controls provided in the portal or ask an administrator if your status needs to be corrected.

### 3.2 View your schedule

Open **Schedule** to see your weekly schedule. This is a read-only timetable; teachers cannot edit schedule entries here. Choose a subject filter to focus the view, select a day to expand it, and use **Print** to print the displayed schedule.

The color legend distinguishes schedule types, such as lecture, laboratory, consultation, campus activity, lunch, and office hours. Use the displayed work-hours row and timetable entries to confirm the applicable day and time.

### 3.3 Review and manage consultation requests

Open **Consultation** to review requests. Use the status tabs and counts to filter the list. Select a request card to expand its details, including the student, subject, reason, description, requested time, and queue information.

- **Approve:** approve an eligible pending request. Confirm the approval when prompted. Where available, **Approve All** processes all incoming pending requests.
- **Reschedule:** choose this when the requested appointment cannot be honored. Enter a reason (required) and confirm. The student can then respond to the proposed change or make another request.
- **Mark as Done:** after an approved consultation, mark it complete. Add optional notes if helpful; the student can view the notes from the completed session.
- **Update Status:** use the status editor when a request needs a supported status correction.

Queue positions and ticket numbers help track approved and pending sessions. For requests without a scheduled slot, follow the actions and queue controls shown on the request; some actions only appear when the request is eligible.

### 3.4 Events and account settings

Open **Events** to view school events. Teachers can review published event information but do not manage the event catalog from this page.

Open **Settings** to change your password, manage email verification, or read the settings FAQs. Open your profile from the avatar to review or update the profile fields available to your account.

## 4. Administrators

Administrator actions may affect many users. Check the selected term, schedule target, account, or event before saving a change.

### 4.1 Dashboard and activity logs

The dashboard provides access to the main management areas and at-a-glance system information. Open **Activity Logs** to review recorded system activity. Use the filters and controls displayed on that page to narrow the log and inspect relevant entries.

### 4.2 Academic terms — create, edit, publish, and export

Open **Academic Terms** to manage term setup. The term list can be filtered to **All terms**, **Published**, or **Unpublished**. A published term is visible to teachers and students; an unpublished term is restricted to the admin workspace. Only one term is designated as the current published term.

#### Create or edit a term

1. Select **New Term**, or choose **Edit details** on an existing term.
2. **Term details:** choose a school year and semester. The school-year list is limited to the current or a future school year.
3. **Sections:** enter a section count for each year level. Enter **0** where that year has no sections. Name the generated sections if the default names should be changed.
4. **Available rooms:** select at least one room. Use **Select all rooms**, a floor-level **Select all**, or individual room checkboxes.
5. Review the summary and select **Create term** or **Save changes**.

Use **Back** to revisit a step. You can edit these details later. When editing, check whether section or room changes still match the schedules already assigned to the term.

#### Publish a term

Select **Publish term** on the term that should become current, then confirm the action if prompted. Publishing makes the term visible to teachers and students and may prompt students to enter their enrollment type, year, and section. Confirm the term and its sections/rooms before publishing; publishing is distinct from merely creating a term.

#### Browse schedules for a term

Choose **View schedules** on a term. Choose a view mode:

- **Student:** choose a year level, then a section.
- **Room:** search or browse the rooms assigned to the term.
- **Faculty:** search or browse teachers.

Use the search field and paging controls to find a group. Preview cards summarize the schedules. Select **Open schedule** to inspect the full timetable. In the schedule browser, use the available schedule-type filters to narrow the displayed entries.

#### Download term schedules

Select **Download Excel** on a term to export its schedules. The workbook contains grouped schedule views (by room, student group, and faculty), a consolidated schedule-data sheet, and a color legend. Open the relevant sheet in Excel or a compatible spreadsheet application.

### 4.3 View schedules and add schedule entries

The admin schedule area supports a high-level teacher grid and term-specific schedule management:

- **View Schedules** opens the teacher schedule grid. Choose a teacher and optionally filter by section or year. Use the consultation-hours control for a selected teacher, and **Print** or **Excel** to print/export the displayed data.
- **Academic Terms → View schedules** opens a particular term’s schedule browser.
- **Academic Terms → Add schedule** opens the term-specific workflow for adding entries.

In **Add Schedule**, choose a view:

1. Select **Timetable View** to work with a weekly grid, or **Listed View** to browse entries and add from the list.
2. In timetable mode, choose the target type: **Student**, **Room**, or **Faculty**.
3. Choose the student year and section, the room, or the faculty member. For rooms, choose the floor first if required. Search controls can help locate the target.
4. Add or edit schedule entries using the fields shown for that target. Check the selected academic term and target before saving.
5. Review the updated timetable or list for accuracy.

When building a timetable, check the target teacher, room, and student section carefully so an entry is not assigned to the wrong resource or group. Teachers’ own schedule pages are read-only.

### 4.4 Teacher assignments

Open **Teachers** to review faculty status and manage teacher records. Select a status tab—such as **On School**, **On Event**, **On Meeting**, **On Leave**, or **Offline**—to filter the directory. Use **Add teacher** when permitted to create a teacher record.

Teacher cards expose the controls available to administrators, including status, assigned area, and substitute/coverage information. Change a status only after confirming it reflects the teacher’s current situation. Availability may depend on the status; the interface indicates when an action is unavailable. Use the coverage controls to review or manage coverage for the relevant teacher.

For consultation hours, open **View Schedules**, select the teacher, and choose the consultation-hours control. Maintain the hours that students should see when requesting a subject-teacher consultation.

### 4.5 Events management

Open **Events** to create and manage announcements:

1. Select **Add Event** and complete the event form. Provide the title and the event information requested, such as description, date, time, and location. Add a cover image or other optional details if offered.
2. Save the event and confirm it appears in the appropriate list.
3. Use **Edit** on an active event to correct its information.
4. Use **Archive** to remove an event from the active list while retaining it in **Archived**. Restore it from the archived list if needed.
5. Review **Ended** events separately from active and archived events.

Confirm the event date and time before saving. Students and teachers see the published event information in their Events pages.

### 4.6 User management

Open **Users** to manage accounts. The summary cards can filter the user list by total users, administrators, teachers, and student year level. Use the status and role filters and search controls to locate a record.

- Select **Add User** (if available) to register an account. Choose the appropriate role and enter the required identifier and account details. Teacher records use an employee ID; student records use a school ID. Student records may include year/section enrollment details.
- Select **Edit** on a record to correct its account information or status.
- Use the status filter to distinguish **Active**, **Inactive**, and **Archived** accounts.
- Use **Archive** for accounts that should no longer be active in the normal list. Review the confirmation carefully; archived records can be restored from the archived view.
- Use the password reset action when an account holder needs a reset. Confirm the intended account before proceeding.

Account creation and changes are permission-dependent. Use the least access needed for the account’s role.

### 4.7 Admin profile and settings

Open your avatar or **Settings** to manage your own profile and account security. Use the password and email-verification controls described in the next section. **Activity Logs** provide a way to review recorded system actions.

## 5. Account, security, and common help

### Change or reset a password

When signed in, open **Settings** and enter your current password, the new password, and its confirmation. Depending on the portal, the system may require a six-digit email OTP: request the code, retrieve it from your email, enter it, and finish the update.

If you cannot sign in, select **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page:

1. Enter the email address linked to your account and request a code.
2. Enter the six-digit OTP sent to your email. The code expires after five minutes; request a new code if it expires.
3. Set and confirm a new password that meets the displayed requirements.

### Email verification

The **Email verification** toggle controls whether a verification code is sent to your email when you log in. Changing the setting requires your current password; follow any confirmation prompt and check your email if a code is requested.

### Recent devices

Open **Recent devices** from your profile to review devices that have signed in to your account. If you see a device you do not recognize, change your password and contact your administrator.

### Troubleshooting

- **I cannot sign in:** confirm the email and password, check the spelling of your email, and complete any verification prompt. Use **Forgot Password?** if you need a reset.
- **I did not receive an OTP:** verify the account email, check spam/junk folders, and request a new code. For a password-reset code, use it within five minutes.
- **I cannot see a schedule:** students may need to complete the enrollment prompt for the published term. Teachers should contact an administrator if the assigned schedule is missing or incorrect.
- **A term, section, teacher, or room is missing:** ask an administrator to review the term setup, account assignment, or schedule record.
- **A consultation option is unavailable:** the teacher may not be accepting requests, may have no consultation hours configured, or the action may not apply to that request’s current status.
- **A page reports a loading or saving error:** retry once, check your network connection, and avoid submitting the same change repeatedly. If it continues, contact the system administrator and include the page name and the action that failed.

For account access, incorrect records, missing options, or persistent errors, contact your CIT Scheduler administrator. Do not share your password or verification codes with anyone.
